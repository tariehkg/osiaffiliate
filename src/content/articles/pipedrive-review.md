## What Pipedrive is

Pipedrive is the pick for people whose day is a pipeline. It opens on stages and deals rather than a dashboard, and its whole structure pushes one question at you: what is the next action on this deal, and when.

## What it does well

That focus is the reason it made the list over several broader tools. Reps update it because updating it is how they see their own work, which is the only reliability test a CRM really has.

- **Outbound and follow-up sales.** Outbound and follow-up sales is where Pipedrive earns its place on our CRM & Sales shortlist.
- **Reps who own a number.** Reps who own a number is where Pipedrive earns its place on our CRM & Sales shortlist.
- **Visual deal stages.** Visual deal stages is where Pipedrive earns its place on our CRM & Sales shortlist.

## Where it falls short

Skip it if you need marketing email, a help desk or a free tier. Pipedrive is deliberately a sales tool, so the rest of the customer lifecycle ends up in other subscriptions.

## How Pipedrive pricing works

Paid per seat on tiered plans, from a basic pipeline up to tiers that add automation, reporting and forecasting, with a handful of optional add-ons for things like lead capture and campaigns.

No lasting free plan; a time-limited trial of the paid product, with no card needed to start it.

## Pipedrive alternatives

- **[HubSpot CRM](/hubspot-crm-review)** — broadest free tier; grows into marketing and service. Free plan available.
- **[Close](/close-review)** — calling and email built in, for teams that sell by phone. Free trial, then paid per seat.
- **[monday CRM](/monday-crm-review)** — sales boards for teams already on monday.com. Free trial, then paid per seat.
