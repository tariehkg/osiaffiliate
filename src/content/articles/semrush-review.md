## What Semrush is

Semrush is on this list because it is the closest thing to a single marketing-visibility toolkit: keyword research, competitor comparison, site audits, rank tracking, local listings and paid-search data in one subscription.

## What it does well

For a small team with one person doing all of SEO, that breadth beats a better single-purpose tool. You can answer “what should we write”, “why did that page drop” and “what are they bidding on” without three logins and three invoices.

- **One person doing all SEO.** One person doing all SEO is where Semrush earns its place on our SEO & Marketing Tools shortlist.
- **Agency reporting.** Agency reporting is where Semrush earns its place on our SEO & Marketing Tools shortlist.
- **Competitor research.** Competitor research is where Semrush earns its place on our SEO & Marketing Tools shortlist.

## Where it falls short

Skip it if you only need one of these jobs done. Buying the whole toolkit for backlink data or rank tracking alone is the most expensive way to get either.

## How Semrush pricing works

Tiered subscriptions sold as Pro, Guru and Business that raise project, keyword and report limits as you move up, with additional users and specialist toolkits such as local or content sold as add-ons.

A lasting free account that lets you run a limited number of lookups and one small project, enough to look something up but not to work in.

## Semrush alternatives

- **[Ahrefs](/ahrefs-review)** — strongest backlink and referring-domain data. Paid plans; free Webmaster Tools for your own site.
- **[Moz Pro](/moz-pro-review)** — friendlier learning curve; good rank tracking. Free trial, then paid monthly.
