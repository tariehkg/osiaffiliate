## What Asana is

Asana is the pick for teams who would rather inherit a process than design one. Projects, tasks, subtasks, assignees and due dates work the obvious way on day one, and a new person can be productive in it without a training session.

## What it does well

That opinionated structure is the feature. It means less configuration, fewer arguments about how to use the tool, and a shape that holds up as the team grows — which is exactly where more flexible tools tend to sag.

- **Clear ownership and deadlines.** Clear ownership and deadlines is where Asana earns its place on our Project Management & Productivity shortlist.
- **No build phase.** No build phase is where Asana earns its place on our Project Management & Productivity shortlist.
- **Growing teams.** Growing teams is where Asana earns its place on our Project Management & Productivity shortlist.

## Where it falls short

Skip it if you want a docs-and-database workspace you can reshape, or your work does not fit tasks-with-dates. Asana is confident about how projects should look, and bending it is more work than leaving it.

## How Asana pricing works

A free plan for small teams and tiered paid plans priced per user, with timeline, reporting, workload and advanced automation arriving as you move up the tiers.

A lasting free plan for a small team covering projects, tasks, list, board and calendar views.
