## What GetResponse is

GetResponse made the list because it collapses three subscriptions into one account: email sending, landing pages, and the automation that connects them. For a solo operator, that is not a feature list — it is one bill and one place where a funnel breaks.

## What it does well

The automation builder is genuinely capable without demanding a week of study, and the free plan is real enough to build and send from while you are still working out whether the business needs any of this.

- **Lead capture end to end.** Lead capture end to end is where GetResponse earns its place on our Email Marketing & Automation shortlist.
- **One tool, one bill.** One tool, one bill is where GetResponse earns its place on our Email Marketing & Automation shortlist.
- **Solo marketers.** Solo marketers is where GetResponse earns its place on our Email Marketing & Automation shortlist.

## Where it falls short

Skip it if you already pay for a website or page builder you like. Half of what you are buying here is page and funnel tooling, and a pure email platform will do the sending part with less around it.

## How GetResponse pricing works

Tiered plans that scale with the size of your contact list, with the higher tiers adding automation depth, funnels, webinars and ecommerce features.

A lasting free plan that lets you build a list, design a landing page and send newsletters to a small audience without a card.

## GetResponse alternatives

- **[AWeber](/aweber-review)** — long-running, simple list tooling with human support. Free plan available.
- **[ActiveCampaign](/activecampaign-review)** — the deepest automation builder of the three. Free trial, then paid by contacts.
