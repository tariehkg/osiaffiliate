## What Close is

Close made the list for one specific shape of team: the kind that sells on the phone. Calling, SMS and email sequences are built into the CRM rather than bolted on through a dialer integration, so the call happens where the record lives and the logging is not a separate habit.

## What it does well

For a team doing real outbound volume, that removes the failure point that kills most CRM rollouts — the gap between doing the work and recording it.

- **Inside sales teams.** Inside sales teams is where Close earns its place on our CRM & Sales shortlist.
- **Daily outbound calling.** Daily outbound calling is where Close earns its place on our CRM & Sales shortlist.
- **Fewer tools in the loop.** Fewer tools in the loop is where Close earns its place on our CRM & Sales shortlist.

## Where it falls short

Skip it if your sales are inbound, self-serve or handled over email and chat. You would be paying a premium for a dialer nobody picks up.

## How Close pricing works

Paid per seat on tiered plans, with the built-in phone system included rather than sold separately; calling usage itself is metered on top.

No free plan; a time-limited trial of the full product so you can test the dialer against your own list.
