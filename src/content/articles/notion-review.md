## What Notion is

Notion is on this list because it removes the wall between the document and the tracker. A project brief, the task list it produced and the notes from the call about it can be the same object, which is where most tool-switching friction actually comes from.

## What it does well

It is also the most adaptable pick here, and that is the whole risk. Notion gives you databases and views and no opinion about how to run a project, so it works brilliantly when one person owns the structure and drifts into a junk drawer when nobody does.

- **Docs and work together.** Docs and work together is where Notion earns its place on our Project Management & Productivity shortlist.
- **One person owns the setup.** One person owns the setup is where Notion earns its place on our Project Management & Productivity shortlist.
- **Shaping your own system.** Shaping your own system is where Notion earns its place on our Project Management & Productivity shortlist.

## Where it falls short

Skip it if you want the tool to impose a process, or you need heavy dependency, workload and resourcing management. Notion will happily let you build something unmaintainable.

## How Notion pricing works

A free plan and tiered paid plans priced per member, with the higher tiers adding admin controls, larger guest allowances and more history, and AI features sold as an add-on or bundled depending on plan.

A lasting free plan that is fully usable for an individual and enough for a small team to find out whether Notion suits them.

## Notion alternatives

- **[ClickUp](/clickup-review)** — most features per seat, if you can stand the settings. Free plan available.
- **[Asana](/asana-review)** — opinionated task and project structure out of the box. Free plan available.
