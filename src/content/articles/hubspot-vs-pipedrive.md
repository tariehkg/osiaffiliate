## Our verdict

If you want the short version: *pipeline discipline points to Pipedrive, one customer record points to HubSpot*. Most teams know which of those sentences describes them, and that is a better basis for the decision than a feature count.

The trap worth naming is the free plan. HubSpot free is the easiest way to get a CRM running this week, and it is genuinely good, so plenty of teams end up there by default and then discover that the features making HubSpot worth it sit a tier or two up. That is fine if breadth was the plan. It is an expensive accident if all you ever wanted was a pipeline — in which case paying Pipedrive from month one is the cheaper decision over three years, not the dearer one.

Both will hold your data well. The difference in how hard they are to leave is real though: HubSpot gets stickier the more of your marketing lives inside it, and that is worth pricing in while the decision is still cheap to make.

## What this comparison does not cover

We have not run a full sales year in both tools side by side, so treat the day-to-day judgements as informed reading of the products and their documentation rather than a field test. Specific plan prices, seat minimums and contact tiers are deliberately absent because both vendors change them; check each pricing page before you commit.
