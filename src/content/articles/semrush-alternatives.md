## Why people look for a Semrush alternative

Skip Semrush if you only need one of these jobs done. Buying the whole toolkit for backlink data or rank tracking alone is the most expensive way to get either.

- **You only need one job done.** This is the most common reason a reader writes to us about leaving Semrush.
- **Backlinks or ranks alone.** This is the most common reason a reader writes to us about leaving Semrush.

## The 2 best Semrush alternatives

### 1. Ahrefs: Strongest backlink and referring-domain data

Ahrefs earns its slot on link data. Its index of backlinks and referring domains is the strongest of the tools here, and if your work involves digital PR, link building or working out why a competitor outranks you on authority rather than content, that is the number you live in.

The interface is unusually direct for the category: paste a domain, get the picture. Less time spent assembling a report is a real feature when the report is weekly.

**Choose Ahrefs over Semrush if** link-led growth plans, digital pr and outreach, seo specialists and agencies matter more to you than one person doing all seo. No general free plan; free Webmaster Tools give audit and backlink data for sites you can verify as your own. [Read our Ahrefs review](/ahrefs-review) or <a href="https://ahrefs.com" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">visit ahrefs.com</a>.

### 2. Moz Pro: Friendlier learning curve; good rank tracking

Moz Pro is on this list because it is the gentlest entry point to SEO tooling that still does real work. Rank tracking, site crawls and keyword research are laid out for someone who is learning the discipline, not someone who already thinks in link graphs.

It comes with the clearest teaching material in the category, which matters more than it sounds: most small businesses do not need a better tool, they need to understand the one they have.

**Choose Moz Pro over Semrush if** first serious seo tool, learning the discipline, consistent rank tracking matter more to you than one person doing all seo. No lasting free plan; a time-limited trial of the full product, plus free limited tools on the Moz site for quick lookups. [Read our Moz Pro review](/moz-pro-review) or <a href="https://moz.com" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">visit moz.com</a>.

## When Semrush is still the right choice

Best for in-house marketers and agencies who need to cover several jobs and report on them. The free account is real but narrow — enough to look something up, not enough to work in.
