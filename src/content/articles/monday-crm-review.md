## What monday CRM is

monday CRM is on this list with an honest condition attached: it is the right answer mainly if your team already runs on monday.com. Then the sales pipeline becomes another board in the tool everyone already opens, with the same automations, views and permissions you have already learned.

## What it does well

That shared-surface advantage is real and it is the reason it beats a second, better CRM for these teams. One login, one place where work lives, no arguing about which tool holds the truth about a customer.

- **Existing monday.com teams.** Existing monday.com teams is where monday CRM earns its place on our CRM & Sales shortlist.
- **Sales next to delivery.** Sales next to delivery is where monday CRM earns its place on our CRM & Sales shortlist.
- **One login for work.** One login for work is where monday CRM earns its place on our CRM & Sales shortlist.

## Where it falls short

Skip it if you are not already a monday.com team. Judged purely as a CRM against Pipedrive or HubSpot it is the less purposeful tool, and adopting the whole work platform to get it is a much bigger decision than picking a CRM.

## How monday CRM pricing works

Paid per seat on tiered plans, alongside monday.com’s other products, with the higher tiers unlocking more automations, integrations and reporting.

No lasting free plan for the CRM; a time-limited trial lets you build boards and test the pipeline before paying.

## monday CRM alternatives

- **[HubSpot CRM](/hubspot-crm-review)** — broadest free tier; grows into marketing and service. Free plan available.
- **[Pipedrive](/pipedrive-review)** — pipeline-first, built around the next action on a deal. Free trial, then paid per seat.
- **[Close](/close-review)** — calling and email built in, for teams that sell by phone. Free trial, then paid per seat.
