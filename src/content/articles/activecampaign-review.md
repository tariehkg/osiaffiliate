## What ActiveCampaign is

ActiveCampaign earns its slot on automation depth. Its builder handles conditional branches, waits, scoring and goal-based paths comfortably, and it is the tool on this shortlist most likely to still fit once your follow-up logic gets genuinely complicated.

## What it does well

It also has a real CRM side, so sales follow-up and marketing email can sit against the same contact rather than being synced between two products.

- **Complex automation logic.** Complex automation logic is where ActiveCampaign earns its place on our Email Marketing & Automation shortlist.
- **Behaviour-triggered follow-up.** Behaviour-triggered follow-up is where ActiveCampaign earns its place on our Email Marketing & Automation shortlist.
- **Email and CRM together.** Email and CRM together is where ActiveCampaign earns its place on our Email Marketing & Automation shortlist.

## Where it falls short

Skip it if you send one newsletter a week. The builder that justifies the price is the one you would never open, and the learning curve is the steepest of the three.

## How ActiveCampaign pricing works

Tiered plans priced by the number of contacts on your list, with marketing and sales bundles sold separately or together and the higher tiers adding predictive and multi-user features.

No free plan; a time-limited trial of the platform with no card required, enough to build and test a real automation.

## ActiveCampaign alternatives

- **[GetResponse](/getresponse-review)** — email plus landing pages and funnels in one account. Free plan available.
- **[AWeber](/aweber-review)** — long-running, simple list tooling with human support. Free plan available.
