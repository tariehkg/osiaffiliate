## Why people look for a Notion alternative

Skip Notion if you want the tool to impose a process, or you need heavy dependency, workload and resourcing management. Notion will happily let you build something unmaintainable.

- **You want an imposed process.** This is the most common reason a reader writes to us about leaving Notion.
- **Heavy resourcing needs.** This is the most common reason a reader writes to us about leaving Notion.

## The 2 best Notion alternatives

### 1. ClickUp: Most features per seat, if you can stand the settings

ClickUp made the list on sheer coverage. Tasks, docs, goals, time tracking, whiteboards and a stack of views come in one product, including on a free plan that is unusually complete for the category.

If you have a specific requirement — a particular view, a custom field, an automation, a report — ClickUp usually has it. That is a genuine advantage when you are replacing three tools and cannot afford a gap.

**Choose ClickUp over Notion if** replacing several tools, maximum capability per seat, teams with a configurer matter more to you than docs and work together. A lasting free plan that is unusually complete for the category, covering tasks, docs, several views and basic automation. [Read our ClickUp review](/clickup-review) or <a href="https://clickup.com" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">visit clickup.com</a>.

### 2. Asana: Opinionated task and project structure out of the box

Asana is the pick for teams who would rather inherit a process than design one. Projects, tasks, subtasks, assignees and due dates work the obvious way on day one, and a new person can be productive in it without a training session.

That opinionated structure is the feature. It means less configuration, fewer arguments about how to use the tool, and a shape that holds up as the team grows — which is exactly where more flexible tools tend to sag.

**Choose Asana over Notion if** clear ownership and deadlines, no build phase, growing teams matter more to you than docs and work together. A lasting free plan for a small team covering projects, tasks, list, board and calendar views. [Read our Asana review](/asana-review) or <a href="https://asana.com" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">visit asana.com</a>.

## When Notion is still the right choice

Best for small teams, agencies and solo operators who want their docs and their work in one place and are willing to design the system once. The free plan is enough to find out whether you are that kind of team.
