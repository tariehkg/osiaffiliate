# Site Taxonomy — OSI Affiliate

**Positioning:** A curated software directory for small businesses, solopreneurs, and marketers choosing the tools to run and grow their business — not an open marketplace listing every vendor who signs up, but a short, opinionated shortlist per category, backed by real comparison depth.

**Category selection rationale:** picked from the niche scan for the combination of strong, well-documented recurring-commission affiliate programs and genuinely high, evergreen search demand — not an attempt to match Capterra/G2's 80+ category breadth.

## Primary Categories

### 1. CRM & Sales Software
**Buyer question it answers:** "Which CRM should I actually use to manage leads and close deals?"
**Why it made the cut:** The single highest-demand B2B software search category (G2 shows 1,600+ listings under CRM alone); strong recurring-commission programs exist (HubSpot ~30%/12mo, Pipedrive, Close).
**Page types:** Category hub + 1 head-to-head comparison (the two most-searched contenders)

### 2. Email Marketing & Automation
**Buyer question it answers:** "Which email platform actually gets emails delivered and automates follow-up?"
**Why it made the cut:** Some of the strongest commission rates found in the whole scan (GetResponse 40-60% recurring, AWeber 30-50% recurring, ManyChat 50% with a 120-day cookie).
**Page types:** Category hub + 1 head-to-head comparison

### 3. Website & Funnel Builders
**Buyer question it answers:** "What should I actually build my site or sales funnel on?"
**Why it made the cut:** High one-time and recurring commissions (Systeme.io 60% lifetime, Webflow 50% of first-year revenue) plus consistently massive "best website builder" search volume.
**Page types:** Category hub + 1 head-to-head comparison

### 4. Project Management & Productivity
**Buyer question it answers:** "What tool keeps my team's work organized without becoming its own full-time job?"
**Why it made the cut:** G2's own data shows this as the single most-browsed category on their homepage; Notion's 50%/12mo program is one of the stronger mainstream-tool rates found.
**Page types:** Category hub only at launch (no comparison page yet — revisit once traffic data shows real "X vs Y" demand)

### 5. SEO & Marketing Tools
**Buyer question it answers:** "What tool actually helps me get found in search and understand what's working?"
**Why it made the cut:** Attracts a high-intent, higher-budget professional buyer; strong recurring commissions and the longest cookie window found in the scan (Semrush, 120 days).
**Page types:** Category hub only at launch

## Silo Map
Each category hub is a **pillar** page (the page most likely to rank for "best [category] software"). Its comparison page, where one exists, is a **cluster** page that ranks for narrower "X vs Y" searches and links back up to its pillar. The homepage links out to all 5 pillars; pillars link down to their cluster and back up to the homepage and to `/how-we-pick`.
